How it works

From “I'm craving something” to a recipe that suits you.

  1. Track your cycle

    Log your period and how you feel in a few taps. CraveIt learns your cycle length as you go and tells you how confident its predictions are.

  2. Say what you're craving

    Sweet, salty, crunchy, creamy, or one ingredient you can't stop thinking about — plus how much time you have and which meal it's for.

  3. Get recipes for your phase

    Recipes are ranked for your craving, your current phase, your goals and the diet and allergy settings you've chosen — and each one shows why it was picked.

Cravings, taken seriously

Your craving is the starting point, not the problem.

Many people notice their cravings change across the month. Instead of fighting them, CraveIt starts from them: tell it what you feel like and it suggests meals that match the craving and suit where you are in your cycle.

Four phases, one cycle

See which phase you're most likely in, at a glance.

Your home screen shows your likely phase and cycle day, and your calendar marks what you've logged and what's only predicted.

  • Period

    Your cycle starts on the first day of bleeding.

  • Follicular

    The days after your period, leading up to ovulation.

  • Ovulation

    A short window around the middle of your cycle.

  • Luteal

    The days before your next period begins.

What's inside

Built around you, not an average.

  • A calendar that learns you

    Predictions improve with every cycle you log, and the app shows when it's unsure instead of pretending to know.

  • Over 5,000 recipes

    With South African ingredient names. Set your allergies and diet, and CraveIt hides recipes whose listed ingredients don't fit. Ingredient information can be incomplete, so if you have a food allergy, always check the ingredients and product labels yourself.

  • Your goals, your conditions

    Tell CraveIt about your goals or a health condition you live with, such as PCOS or endometriosis, and it takes them into account when it ranks recipes. This is general nutrition support only — it does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

  • Sources you can check

    Tap the info icon beside cycle guidance to see the research behind it.

  • Badges and streaks

    Small wins for logging, cooking and trying something new, collected as you go.

  • A weekly report

    Your week's cycle, cravings and meals in one place, so patterns are easier to spot.
