Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 · The CraveIt app shows this policy. The erasure email in section 5 was added on 21 September 2026.

Who we are
Crave It Health (Pty) Ltd, company registration number 2026/078218/07, Cape Town, South Africa. We make the CraveIt app.
How to reach us
support@craveit.co.za

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information (email, username)
  • Health data (cycle information, symptoms)
  • Dietary preferences and goals
  • Usage data and analytics

2. How we use your information

  • Provide personalized cycle tracking
  • Offer tailored recipe recommendations
  • Improve our services
  • Send important updates

3. Data security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your health information is stored securely and privately.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate the app, and they are bound by confidentiality agreements.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time through the app settings.

To ask us to erase your data — including if you can no longer sign in — email support@craveit.co.za. Step-by-step instructions are on our support page.

This website

This note is about craveit.co.za itself, not the app. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

If you email us to join the waiting list, we use your address only to tell you when CraveIt is available, and we'll delete it if you ask.
